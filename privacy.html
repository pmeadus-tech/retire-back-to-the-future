Privacy Policy

from The Crude Dude · Effective date: November 9, 2026

This policy is written in plain language. It is not legal advice. It covers the web version of the Retire Back to the Future Calculator and the phone app (when it launches). Run by The Crude Dude, an Alberta business.

The short version

  • Web version: nothing leaves your device. Your numbers and photos stay in this browser (localStorage and IndexedDB). We do not get a copy.
  • Phone app: we store your email, birthdate, the confirmed numbers you save, and your feedback, with our database provider (Supabase) in Canada — region pending confirmation before launch. Photos never upload.
  • No ads. No analytics trackers. We do not sell your information.
  • Questions or requests: contact@crudedude.ca.

1. Web version (this site)

On the web calculator:

  • What is stored: the numbers you type, optional feedback answers, and any pay-stub / bill / receipt / benefits photos or PDFs you choose to open.
  • Where: only on your device, in this browser's localStorage and IndexedDB.
  • OCR and PDF reading run on your device using software bundled with the page.
  • We do not receive your photos, OCR text, or calculator numbers from the web version.
  • Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes it. There is no Crude Dude account on the web version.

2. Phone app (when live)

When you create an account in the phone app, we collect:

  • Email — login, confirmation and password-reset links only (not marketing from the app).
  • Birthdate — TFSA room and retirement countdown; locked after sign-up so the math stays steady.
  • Confirmed calculator numbers — what you save after you check it (unconfirmed OCR guesses stay on the phone).
  • Feedback answers — “Tell grandpa what you think,” optional.

Photos and documents never leave your phone. Pay stubs, bills, receipts and benefits booklets are read on the device. We never receive the images.

Account data is stored with Supabase in Canada (exact region to be confirmed before launch and named here). If that ever changes, we will say so, including if information is stored outside Canada.

3. What we do not collect

We do not collect your SIN, bank or CRA passwords, advertising IDs, contacts, or location. The web version and the app have no ads and no analytics pixels.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Web: data stays until you clear it in the browser or remove site data.
  • Phone app: kept while you have an account. Delete in-app (Account → Delete my account) or email contact@crudedude.ca from the account email with subject Delete my account. Live account data is deleted promptly; backup copies clear within 30 days. See also Delete account.

5. Your rights (PIPEDA and Alberta PIPA)

We follow Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and, where personal information is handled in interprovincial or international commercial activity, federal PIPEDA. You can ask what we hold, ask for a correction, withdraw consent (in practice by deleting your account), or complain to us or to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca) or Alberta's OIPC (oipc.ab.ca). We answer within 30 days.

6. Breaches

If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify affected people and the required regulators. We keep an internal breach response plan.
