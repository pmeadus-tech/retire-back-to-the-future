8. RRSP room

Check your Notice of Assessment or CRA My Account for your real RRSP number.

Grandpa's take, from the Crude Dude TFSA vs RRSP research: TFSA first for most young workers and anybody in a lower tax bracket. The RRSP gets more valuable once you're in the higher brackets, 30.5% and up combined in Alberta. And if the company matches a group RRSP, take that first. Arguing with free money is how you stay broke. RRSP money for a tax year usually has to go in by about March 1 of the next year. RRSP room is not TFSA room; they're two separate buckets.

Get your exact room from the CRA

My numbers are a good guess. The CRA keeps the real count, and you can look it up yourself, kiddo.

Sign in to your CRA account (or sign up for one)

  1. Sign in, or hit "Register" if you've never set one up.
  2. Pick Individual account.
  3. Click Savings and pension plans, then View TFSA details.
  4. Click Contribution room. That's your number.
Heads up: CRA's number is your room as of January 1. It won't count anything you've put in this year, and last year's moves only show up after CRA's spring update (around April). Take this year's deposits off it yourself before you put more in.

Crew non-negotiables

  1. The plan isn't hard. Sticking to it is.
  2. Buy the truck that fits the budget, not the one with $15,000 of options, and pay it off before the warranty runs out.
  3. That truck sits in the driveway more than you drive it. Remember that at the dealership.
  4. Overtime goes to the TFSA first. You never lived on it, so you won't miss it.
  5. Don't overcontribute. Going over your room costs 1% a month on the extra until it's out.
  6. Take money out and you get the room back on January 1 next year, not the same year.
