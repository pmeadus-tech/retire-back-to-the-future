Delete your account

from The Crude Dude · Effective date: November 9, 2026

This page is for the phone app account (Google Play / App Store requirement). The web calculator has no account — just clear this site's data in your browser to remove local numbers and photos.

Delete in the app

  1. Open the Retirement Backcast Calculator app.
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm.

That deletes your account and everything saved to it, and clears the app's data on that phone (including on-device photos in the app's storage).

Delete by email

Email hello@crudedude.ca from the email address on the account, with the subject line:

Delete my account

We will delete the account within 30 days and confirm when it is done. We may ask a short question to make sure it is really you.

What gets deleted

  • Your login (email), birthdate, saved calculator numbers, and feedback answers in our live database.
  • Copies in routine backups clear automatically within 30 days.
  • Photos were never uploaded; deleting the app / clearing app data removes them from that phone.

What deleting the app alone does not do

Uninstalling the app from your phone does not delete the cloud account. Use in-app delete or the email request above.
